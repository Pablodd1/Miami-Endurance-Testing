TRAINING ZONES

Z1

Recovery

Pure aerobic metabolism. Blood lactate remains at resting levels. Enhances fat oxidation.

Z2

Aerobic Base

Foundation for endurance performance. Mitochondrial biogenesis peaks.

Z3

Tempo

The gray zone. Lactate rises above baseline but is cleared efficiently.

Z4

Threshold

Lactate threshold (LT2). Lactate accumulates faster than cleared.

Z5

VO2max

Maximum anaerobic effort. Body cannot supply oxygen fast enough.
